# TRH LQ Fractal — Setup Drawings

Draws **setups like your images** (position-tool boxes), not signal stickers.

## What you see

| Drawing | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| Soft POI box | Fresh Zone / BB+FVG |
| **Pink risk box** | Entry → Stop (S) |
| **Teal reward box** | Entry → Take Profit |
| **E** / **S** | Proximal entry · distal stop |
| **LQ** | Liquidity under construction |
| Tiny **MSS** / **fs** | Structure tags only |

## Model

- Bias: 4H+W or D+MN  
- Stacks: `1 — 15 — 4H — W` · `5 — 1H — D — MN`  
- A Fresh Zone · B BB+FVG · C fs continuation
