FORGE HELP

Common questions

How do I open a chart?+

Search for a market above and select Open chart beside its name. Launch charts opens the full Forge workspace directly.

How do I save favorite markets?+

Select the star beside a market and switch to Watchlist. This preview saves your selections in this browser.

Where can I manage my profile?+

Use the profile link in the footer to open your Forge profile.
