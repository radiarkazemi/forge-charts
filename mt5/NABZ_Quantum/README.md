## Move the panel (fixed in 2.6)

1. Click the full-width gold bar **⠿ MOVE PANEL**
2. Move your mouse — the panel follows
3. Click **✔ DROP HERE** to lock

You can also **hold left mouse** on that bar and drag.

> Note: the trade-grid **DRAG** button places Entry/SL/TP lines — it does **not** move the panel.
